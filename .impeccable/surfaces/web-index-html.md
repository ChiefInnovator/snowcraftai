# Surface brief: Snowcraft Reforged home page (`web/index.html`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: players who want a snowball fight now, and Rich Crane's audience who want the recovery story; weighted equally. Action: play in the first viewport, then send the page to someone. Proof: the playable game, recovered 1999 sprites beside their redrawn versions. Constraints: game DOM hooks, CSP without inline script, browser test suite, real assets only.

Memorable moment: the game sits tipped into an open holiday card, and sending it on stamps today's date onto the envelope.

Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the studio holiday card that came in the December post, opened on a desk with the game tipped inside. It refuses the browser-game landing page: dark hero, Play button, feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Spruce-green desk blotter as ground; one cranberry card with a white inside leaf; a silver envelope; ink black. Silver brush-script greeting, Clarendon headings and card text, a plain grotesque for small print. Paper pieces with soft cast shadows, corner mounts on every picture, a round postmark, ruled form lines for controls.

STORY: This is the 1999 game, it plays here right now, and it is worth passing on. The visitor plays, reads how it was recovered, and sends it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Envelope tilted behind an open card that nearly fills the screen. Left leaf, cranberry, about 30% wide: the script greeting, the one-line hook, links to the enclosures. Right leaf, white: the playable game at full leaf width with corner mounts, a one-line instruction and the settings as form lines beneath. The game is the primary action.

FORM: The Studio Holiday Card, position 1 on the ordered list, chosen by the user over the roll. seed:352e3cab89912c4f. Signature interaction: Stamp and send, the share control cancels the envelope with a dated postmark. Motion grammar: paper settles once on arrival; enclosures do not animate on scroll.
