How to play

Your team

  1. Point. Move over one of your three kids and a ring appears under them.

  2. Hold. The meter beside them fills in under a second. Drag while you hold to move them.

  3. Let go. Snowballs only fly up and to the left, so you line up by moving.

One hit leaves a kid dazed for a few seconds, and you can’t pick them up. A second hit puts them out.

The green team

They don’t aim. They wander, sidestep and throw down and to the right at random strength, so stay out of their lanes.

Each one takes three hits on Normal, two on Easy and four on Hard. The hit before the last knocks them flat for a moment, and snowballs sail straight over them while they’re down.

Levels 1 to 4 bring 3, 5, 7 and 9 of them. Levels 5 to 10 bring twelve.

On a phone: turn it sideways, touch a kid, hold, slide to move them, and lift your finger to throw.

Where this came from

For the 1999 holidays, Nicholson NY made a small snowball fight in Macromedia Director and signed it off with “Happy Holidays”. Three kids, a diagonal field, and a green team that kept coming.

Adobe retired Shockwave in 2019, and the game went quiet with the plug-in that ran it.

This remake starts from the original file. The ten level layouts, the paths the kids walk in on, 36 sprites and 15 sounds were read straight out of the 1999 game, and the rules were recovered from its scripts. Then it was checked against a playthrough of the original.

What’s new is what 1999 couldn’t do. It runs on a phone, it goes full screen, and the kids have been redrawn at eight times the size if you’d like them that way.

Small print

Can I play full screen?

Yes. Use the button in the top-right corner of the game, or press F. Press Esc, F or the button again to leave. On a phone, full screen turns the game sideways where the browser allows it.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, nothing to buy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on desktop and phones: Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways and the game fills the screen. Touch a kid, hold to wind up, slide to move them, and lift your finger to throw. For the biggest view, tap the full-screen button in the corner of the game; tap it again to leave.

How is the score worked out?

Clearing a level adds one point per green kid on it. If you lose, the kids you hit on that level are added and the total is multiplied by the level number. Clear all ten and your score is multiplied by ten, up to 1000.

What is this based on?

SnowCraft, the 1999 holiday game by Nicholson NY. This remake follows its rules and timing. See the notice. Richard Crane and MILL5 are not affiliated with the original publishers.
