Snowcraft Reforged

Pick a kid. Wind up. Let it fly.

Three kids against the green team. Clear all ten levels.

Turn your phone sideways to play.

How to play

The green team

They don’t aim. They wander, sidestep and throw down and to the right at random strength, so stay out of their lanes.

Each one takes three hits on Normal, two on Easy and four on Hard. The hit before the last knocks them flat for a moment, and snowballs sail straight over them while they’re down.

Levels 1 to 4 bring 3, 5, 7 and 9 kids. Levels 5 to 10 bring twelve.

Your team

  1. Point at one of your three kids. A ring appears under them.
  2. Hold the mouse button. The meter beside them fills in under a second, and you can drag them while you hold.
  3. Let go to throw. Snowballs always fly up and to the left, so line up by moving, not by aiming.

A kid hit once is dazed for a few seconds and can’t be picked up. A second hit knocks them out.

What we’ve added since 1999

The same snowball fight, with modern ways to play. The web edition keeps the recovered game rules; the Flutter edition builds on them.

In your browser

  • Play without installing the original Director game.
  • Touch controls, landscape phone play, full screen and flexible display sizes.
  • Sharp, properly aligned text at every size, including level cards and Play Again.
  • Choose the original artwork or 35 redrawn HD character frames. Scenery and effects use smooth versions of the original art.
  • Independent background music and mute-all settings that remember your choices.
  • Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty, with a shadow that follows the kid you drag.

FAQ

Can I play full screen?

Yes. Use the button in the top-right corner of the game, or press F. Press Esc, F or the button again to leave. On a phone, full screen turns the game sideways where the browser allows it.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, nothing to buy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern desktop browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways and the game fills the screen. Touch a kid, hold to wind up, slide to move them, and lift your finger to throw. For the biggest view, tap the full-screen button in the corner of the game; tap it again to leave.

How is the score worked out?

Clearing a level adds one point per green kid on it. If you lose, the kids you hit on that level are added and the total is multiplied by the level number. Clear all ten and your score is multiplied by ten, up to 1000.
