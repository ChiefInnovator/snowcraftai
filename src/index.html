About the game

Snowcraft Reforged is a free web remake of Snowcraft, the cult 1998 snowball-fight game. It preserves the original sprite art while rebuilding the gameplay on Phaser 3 with 2.5D snowball physics — snowballs have real height, gravity, and arc, so aiming matters.

How to play

  1. Click and hold on any red team player to select them and begin charging throw power.
  2. The power meter fills as you hold — longer holds produce faster, longer-range throws.
  3. Release the mouse button to launch a snowball in an arc toward the green team.
  4. Defeat every green team player to clear the level and advance.
  5. Beat all ten levels to win. The green team throws back, so keep moving.

Features

  • Ten progressive levels, from 3 up to 12 enemies, with a final score.
  • Three difficulty settings: Easy, Normal, and Hard.
  • Scalable display from 600x320 up to 1800x960 for large monitors.
  • Original 1998 Snowcraft sprite assets and sound effects.
  • Modern Phaser 3 physics engine for arc trajectories and collision.
  • Runs in any modern browser on desktop — no install, no signup, no ads.
