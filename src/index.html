Snowcraft Reforged

Pick a kid, hold to wind up, let go to throw. Clear the green team across ten levels.

How to play

The green team

They don’t aim. They wander, sidestep and throw down and to the right at random strength, so stay out of their lanes.

Each one takes three hits on Normal, two on Easy and four on Hard. The hit before the last knocks them flat for a moment, and snowballs sail straight over them while they’re down.

Levels 1 to 4 bring 3, 5, 7 and 9 kids. Levels 5 to 10 bring twelve.

Your team

  1. Point at one of your three kids. A ring appears under them.
  2. Hold the mouse button. The meter beside them fills in under a second, and you can drag them while you hold.
  3. Let go to throw. Snowballs always fly up and to the left, so line up by moving, not by aiming.

A kid hit once is dazed for a few seconds and can’t be picked up. A second hit knocks them out.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, nothing to buy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern desktop browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.

Does it work on a phone?

It’s built for a mouse or trackpad, because you pick a kid by pointing at them before you press.

How is the score worked out?

Clearing a level adds one point per green kid on it. If you lose, the kids you hit on that level are added and the total is multiplied by the level number. Clear all ten and your score is multiplied by ten, up to 1000.
