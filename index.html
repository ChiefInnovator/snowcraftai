About the game

Snowcraft Reforged is a free web remake of Snowcraft, the cult 1998 snowball-fight game. It preserves the original sprite art and the recovered Director throw/audio rules: charge 0–8, throw along the isometric diagonal, two hits to drop a red kid and three for green.

How to play

  1. Click and hold on any red team player to select them and begin charging throw power.
  2. The eight-bar power meter fills as you hold — longer holds throw farther along the diagonal.
  3. Release the mouse button to throw. Red throws go up-left; green throws go down-right.
  4. Defeat every green team player to clear the level and advance.
  5. Red kids are out after two hits, green after three. Greens throw back, so keep moving.

Features

  • Eleven named levels with escalating green-team count.
  • Three difficulty settings: Easy, Normal, and Hard (hit counts stay original).
  • Scalable display from 600x320 up to 1800x960 for large monitors.
  • Original 1998 Snowcraft sprite assets and recovered 8-bit sound effects.
  • Isometric snowball steps recovered from the Director movie.
  • Runs in any modern browser on desktop — no install, no signup, no ads.
